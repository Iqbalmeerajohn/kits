No standard cuts. Yours.

No photo catalogue, no one-size cut. Every appointment starts from your hair, in this order.

First, texture

Every head of hair has its own density and direction.We work with what your hair already does, not against it, so the result lasts.

Then, growth

The way your hair grows decides where the cut falls.The shape keeps its balance as it grows, so it never looks "due for a cut" too soon.

Last, your face

Face shape comes third.It fine-tunes the cut to bring out your best features without dictating it.

Your appointment, in four steps.

A cut you notice after the first wash, and in the way it grows over the following weeks.

Look

Face shape, roots and hair type. Then a suggestion, and always a moment for you to think it over.

Prepare

Wash, conditioner and a head massage. A few quiet minutes before the change.

Cut

Careful and precise. Each step is explained to you while the cut takes shape.

Finish by hand

A few minutes, no blow-dry. The same simple routine you can repeat at home.
